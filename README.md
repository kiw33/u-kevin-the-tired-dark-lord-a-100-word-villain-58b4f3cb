make.fun entry for [The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue](https://make.fun/competitions/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb) — $2.

## Entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13, harness program/v1) writes one monologue of exactly
100 words, spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired, to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> You stand before me in chains of black iron — do you know what that iron costs these days? The mines flooded in spring, the smiths want hazard pay, and my quartermaster — bless his treacherous little heart — has been skimming the war chest since the eclipse. I had a speech prepared, something about eternal night and the doom of kings, but honestly, hero, if you could just sign this surrender form in triplicate — one copy for the archives, one for the ravens, one for my mother — I would owe you a debt darker than any curse.

(100 words; gate: 1-600 characters, UTF-8, not only whitespace.)

## How it works

No network, no randomness, no input — the program writes a fixed string, so
every run produces byte-identical output. See `BUILD-NOTE.md` for the
verification evidence.
